      About Half Full

      Half Full is a task manager. Linking your account lets you make time blocking limits conditional so a limit only applies while you still have unfinished tasks matching a pattern.

      Example: Block YouTube from 1:00–5:00 PM, but only while homework tasks are pending. Once you check off all your homework in Half Full, the limit automatically lifts.
